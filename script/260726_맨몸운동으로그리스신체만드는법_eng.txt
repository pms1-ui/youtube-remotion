So, how did I actually build this physique from home? There are three important things. First one is progressive overload, because a lot of men think that you can't build muscle with body weight training, because you don't have heavy weights to rely on. But your body doesn't care if resistance comes from a machine or your own body weight. It only cares if the challenge increases. More reps, more sets, harder variations, better form, more control, less rest between the sets. You need to give your body a reason to adapt, because that's how it grows. If you constantly do 10 same reps, 10 same pull-ups every workout, how do you expect it to change? You're just going to stay in the same position. Progression matters, and knowing how to progress matters even more.

Second thing is high rep training. High reps are one of the biggest reasons calisthenics transformed my physique, because when you perform hundreds and thousands of quality repetitions over time, you build incredible muscular endurance. You create enormous amount of metabolic stress, which drives the muscle growth, and you build control over your body. You build that athletic, dense, Greek god look. Not just muscles that exist, but muscle that you can actually use in your day-to-day life.
